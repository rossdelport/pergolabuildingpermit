City of Bayswater patio and pergola rules

Short answer

In the City of Bayswater, a patio needs a building permit unless it's 10 m² or less, 2.4 m or lower and not attached to anything. Pergolas and shade sails get a 20 m² limit. The City decides in 10 business days (BA1) or 25 (BA2).

Last updated 26 November 2026

Patio, pergola or shade sail?

  • Patio: a roof that water can't get through, like Colorbond sheeting.
  • Pergola: an open structure with a roof that lets water through, like shade cloth.
  • Shade sail: an open structure with a cover that lets water through.

Not sure which you have? See pergola vs patio.

When you don't need a building permit

In the City of Bayswater, you only skip the permit if all of these apply:

StructureMax areaMax heightAttached to anything?
Patio10 m²2.4 mNo
Pergola20 m²2.4 mNo
Shade sail20 m²2.4 mNo

So a patio attached to your house always needs a building permit, whatever its size. Check yours with the patio approval checker.

How to apply

Fees must be paid when you lodge, or the City won't accept the application.

Already built without a permit?

You can apply for retrospective approval. To see what's already approved on a property, you can lodge a plan search request with the City. See approval for an existing patio.

Planning rules (R-Codes, character areas) can still apply even when no building permit is needed. Call the City on 08 9272 0622.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a permit for a patio in the City of Bayswater?

Yes, unless it's 10 m² or less, no more than 2.4 m high and not attached to any other structure.

Do I need a permit for a pergola in Bayswater?

Not if it's 20 m² or less, no more than 2.4 m high and not attached to anything.

Does an attached patio need a permit in Bayswater?

Yes. To be exempt a patio must not be attached to any other structure, so a patio fixed to the house needs a building permit.

How long does Bayswater take to approve a patio?

Up to 10 business days for a certified (BA1) application and up to 25 business days for an uncertified (BA2) one.
