City of Belmont patio, pergola and shade sail rules

Short answer

In Belmont, every patio needs a building permit unless it's freestanding, under 2.4 m high and under 10 m². A pergola or shade sail is exempt if under 20 m² and 2.4 m. Planning approval is only needed in the front setback, heritage areas, special precincts or near the Swan River.

Last updated 16 November 2026

What Belmont calls each one

  • Patio: an unenclosed structure with a waterproof roof, attached to the house or not.
  • Pergola: an open frame covered in a material that lets water through, or an operable louvred roof.
  • Shade sail: fabric that lets water through, fixed to posts or another structure. A shade sail with a waterproof roof counts as a patio.

Building permit

The City of Belmont needs a building permit for all patios, pergolas and shade sails, except:

StructureNo permit if
PatioFreestanding, under 2.4 m high and under 10 m²
Pergola or shade sail with a houseUnder 20 m² and no more than 2.4 m high

So any attached patio needs a permit, whatever its size. Even without a permit, the structure must still meet the National Construction Code.

Planning (development) approval

Most backyard patios in Belmont don't need planning approval. You need it if the patio, pergola or shade sail:

  • is in the front setback and doesn't meet the standard R-Codes rules, or
  • is in a heritage area under Local Planning Scheme No. 15, or
  • is on a lot in a Special Development Precinct, or
  • is within or next to the Swan River Trust Development Control Area (generally properties near the river).

If you need both, the City won't issue the building permit until planning approval is granted and its conditions are dealt with.

What to lodge

For a building permit:

  • A BA1 (certified, decided in 10 business days, with a BA3 from a registered building surveyor) or BA2 (uncertified, 25 business days).
  • For a BA2: a site plan (at least 1:200) with setbacks, ground levels and easements; elevations and a floor plan (at least 1:100); and structural design certified by a registered structural engineer.
  • The fees, and a Construction Training Fund receipt if the work is over $100,000.

For planning approval: the Application for Approval to Commence Development, a Certificate of Title no more than 6 months old, plans and the fees.

You can lodge online through the City's Planning and Building portal, in person at 215 Wright Street, Cloverdale, or by mail. Questions: 9477 7222.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a permit for a patio in the City of Belmont?

Yes, unless it's freestanding, under 2.4 m high and under 10 m². Attached patios always need a building permit.

Do I need approval for a pergola or shade sail in Belmont?

Not if it goes with a house, is under 20 m² and no more than 2.4 m high. Larger ones need a building permit.

When does a Belmont patio need planning approval?

If it's in the front setback and doesn't meet the R-Codes, in a heritage area, in a Special Development Precinct, or within or next to the Swan River Trust Development Control Area.

Is a louvred roof a patio or a pergola in Belmont?

Belmont treats an operable louvred roof as a pergola.
