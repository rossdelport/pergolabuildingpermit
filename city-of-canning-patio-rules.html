City of Canning patio and pergola rules

Short answer

In the City of Canning, a patio needs a building permit unless it's freestanding, 10 m² or less and 2.4 m or lower. Patios usually sit at least 900 mm from side and rear boundaries, with posts up to 2.7 m high. Pergolas up to 20 m² and 2.4 m high don't need a permit.

Last updated 24 November 2026

Patio or pergola?

The City of Canning calls a structure with a solid roof a patio or verandah. A pergola has an open or open-weave roof, like shade cloth. The rules differ.

No building permit if
Patio or verandahFreestanding, 10 m² or less and 2.4 m high or less (all three)
Pergola20 m² or less and 2.4 m high or less

So any attached patio needs a permit. Exempt structures must still meet the building rules, local laws and planning policies.

Setbacks and height

  • Front: no closer to the street than the R-Codes front setback for your density.
  • Corner lots: at least 1.5 m from the secondary street.
  • Side and rear: at least 900 mm. Or the roof can be 500 mm away with posts on the boundary, if you send a fire performance solution with the application.
  • Height: posts up to 2.7 m.

Open space

You must keep the R-Codes minimum open space. A patio that's open on two sides counts as open space, up to 10% of the site area or 50 m² of roof, whichever is less. Check yours with the open space checker.

What to send

  • BA1: a Certificate of Design Compliance from a registered building surveyor and the plans it lists. Decided within 10 working days.
  • BA2: a 1:200 site plan with boundaries, other buildings and floor level against the house, plus 1:100 floor plans and elevations with structural details. Decided within 25 working days.

You may need a structural engineer to sign off the design. Owner-builders can build a patio. Check drainage easements with the City's Engineering Services first. Building Services is on 1300 422 664.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a permit for a patio in the City of Canning?

Yes, unless it's freestanding, 10 m² or less and no more than 2.4 m high. Every attached patio needs a permit.

How close to the boundary can a patio be in Canning?

At least 900 mm from side and rear boundaries, or 500 mm for the roof with posts on the boundary if you include a fire performance solution.

How high can a patio be in Canning?

Patio posts can be up to 2.7 m high.

Does a pergola need a permit in Canning?

Not if it's 20 m² or less and no more than 2.4 m high.
