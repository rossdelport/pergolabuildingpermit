City of Cockburn patio rules

Short answer

In Cockburn, every attached patio needs a building permit, and a freestanding one does if it's over 10 m² or 2.4 m high. Open pergolas get more room: 20 m². An attached patio must usually be 900 mm from the boundary, and shade sails always need a permit.

Last updated 22 November 2026

Patio or pergola? It changes the rules

The City of Cockburn treats a structure with a solid roof (Colorbond or polycarbonate) as a patio. An open roof or shade cloth makes it a pergola.

StructureBuilding permit needed if
Patio attached to the house, garage or another buildingAlways
Freestanding patioOver 10 m², or over 2.4 m high
Pergola (open or shade cloth roof)Over 20 m², or over 2.4 m high
Shade sailAlways, with a structural engineer's design for the posts, footings and fixings

A shade sail made of waterproof fabric counts as a patio.

Distances Cockburn asks for

  • A freestanding patio must be at least 900 mm from the house.
  • A patio attached to the house must be at least 900 mm from a side or rear boundary, for fire safety.
  • To build closer, you need a fire engineer's certified performance solution, or a fire-rated wall (FRL 60/60/60).

These are building code rules. The R-Codes (planning rules) are separate, and a patio that breaks them, for example by eating into the open space your lot needs, may need planning approval too.

What to lodge

  • A BA1 or BA2 form signed by all owners and the builder.
  • Two copies of a site plan at 1:200 or larger, showing the house, other buildings, setbacks to boundaries and the patio's size.
  • Two copies of elevations and a cross-section at 1:100, showing roof pitch, footing size, how it attaches to the house, the roof sheeting and the height.
  • A floor plan at 1:100 if needed, plus the fee and state levies.

If you need planning approval, get it before you lodge the building permit. Even a patio that doesn't need a building permit can still need planning approval. The City decides a BA1 in 10 business days and a BA2 in 25, and offers free pre-lodgement meetings (9411 3608).

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a permit for a patio in Cockburn?

Yes if it's attached to the house. A freestanding patio needs one if it's over 10 m² or over 2.4 m high.

Do I need a permit for a pergola in Cockburn?

Only if it's over 20 m² or over 2.4 m high, and only if it has an open or shade cloth roof. A solid roof makes it a patio.

How close to the boundary can an attached patio be in Cockburn?

900 mm, unless a fire engineer certifies a closer design or you build a fire-rated wall.

Does a shade sail need a permit in Cockburn?

Yes. The City needs plans and a structural engineer's certification for the posts, footings and fixings.
