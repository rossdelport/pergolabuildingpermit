City of Joondalup patio rules

Short answer

In Joondalup, a patio or louvre roof needs a building permit unless it's standalone, 10 m² or less and 2.4 m or less. Pergolas and standalone shade sails are exempt up to 20 m² and 2.4 m. Patios usually skip planning approval if they're open on two sides and meet the setbacks.

Last updated 14 November 2026

Shade sailStandalone, 20 m² or less, 2.4 m or less
Shade sail attached to a buildingAlways needs a permit

For fire safety, a detached patio or louvre roof must be at least 900 mm from the house, and an attached one at least 900 mm from any boundary (except a boundary to a road or public space). Vergola rooflines should be 900 mm from side and rear boundaries.

Pergolas can go up to the boundary, but must not touch or be fixed to the dividing fence, and footings must stay on your land.

Planning approval for patios

On a house or grouped dwelling lot, a patio usually doesn't need planning approval if:

  • It's open on two or more sides
  • It meets the street setback for your R-Code (see below)
  • It's at least 450 mm from the boundary if no more than 2.7 m high and 10 m long; bigger patios need the full side setback
  • It suits the house if it can be seen from the street
  • You keep enough open space and outdoor living area

Watch out for raised floors: if the patio's finished level is more than 500 mm above natural ground, extra rules apply.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a permit for a patio in Joondalup?

Yes, unless it's standalone, 10 m² or less and 2.4 m or less high. Attached patios need a building permit.

Does a pergola need a permit in Joondalup?

Not if it's 20 m² or less and 2.4 m or less high. It can go to the boundary but must not be fixed to the dividing fence.

How close to the boundary can a patio be in Joondalup?

Without planning approval, at least 450 mm if it's no more than 2.7 m high and 10 m long. For fire safety, an attached patio must be 900 mm from the boundary.

Do I need my neighbour's approval for a patio in Joondalup?

No. Joondalup says neighbour approval isn't needed to apply, but it may notify neighbours if the project could affect them.
