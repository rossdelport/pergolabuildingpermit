City of Kwinana patio and pergola rules

Short answer

In the City of Kwinana, a patio needs a building permit if it's attached to another structure, over 10 m² or over 2.4 m high. A pergola or shade sail needs one if it's over 20 m² or 2.4 m high. Plans must be certified by a structural engineer.

Last updated 23 November 2026

When you need a building permit

StructurePermit needed if
Patio (solid roof, like Colorbond or polycarbonate)It's attached to another structure, over 10 m², or over 2.4 m high
Pergola or shade sail (open or shade-cloth roof)It's over 20 m² or over 2.4 m high

So almost every attached patio in Kwinana needs a permit. A small freestanding patio can skip it if it's 10 m² or less, 2.4 m high or less, and not in a high-wind region. The City suggests keeping a freestanding patio at least 900 mm from the house and 500 mm from the boundary.

Kwinana treats a pergola or shade sail with a solid roof as a patio.

Planning approval

A permit-free patio may still need planning approval, for example if it doesn't meet the R-Codes. If it does, you must get planning approval before you lodge the building permit. Call Building Services if you're not sure.

What to send

  • A BA1 or BA2 form signed by all owners and the builder.
  • A site plan (at least 1:200) showing the house and other buildings, setbacks from boundaries, patio size, and post and downpipe spots.
  • Elevations and a cross-section (at least 1:100) certified by a structural engineer, showing roof pitch, footings, how it fixes to the house, roof sheeting and heights.
  • A floor plan (at least 1:100).
  • For steel frames: engineer details of member sizes, bracing, wind load and fixings. For timber: footing and post connection details and the timber type.

With a BA1 (certified by a private building surveyor) the City has 10 business days to decide. With a BA2 it has 25 business days. You can apply online through Kwinana Connect, or call Building Services on 9439 0208.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a permit for a patio in Kwinana?

Yes, if it's attached to the house or another structure, over 10 m², or over 2.4 m high. Only small freestanding patios are exempt.

Do I need a permit for a pergola in Kwinana?

Only if it's over 20 m² or over 2.4 m high. A pergola with a solid roof counts as a patio.

How long does the City of Kwinana take?

10 business days for a certified (BA1) application and 25 business days for an uncertified (BA2) one.

Does my patio need engineering in Kwinana?

Yes. Elevations and cross-sections must be certified by a practising structural engineer.
