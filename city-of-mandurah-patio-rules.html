City of Mandurah patio and pergola rules

Short answer

In the City of Mandurah, a patio needs a building permit unless it's freestanding, 10 m² or less and 2.4 m or lower. Freestanding pergolas up to 20 m² and 2.4 m are exempt. Planning approval usually isn't needed if you meet the R-Codes, but canal lots can differ.

Last updated 25 November 2026

Patio, pergola or shade sail?

The City of Mandurah calls a structure with a water-resistant roof, open on two or more sides, a patio. An open frame with a permeable roof (or no roof) is a pergola. A shade sail is fabric stretched between anchor points.

When you need a building permit

StructureNo building permit if
PatioFreestanding, 10 m² or less and 2.4 m high or less
PergolaFreestanding, 20 m² or less and 2.4 m high or less
Shade sailFreestanding, 10 m² or less and 2.4 m high or less

Anything bigger, or attached to the house, needs a permit.

Planning approval

  • Patios: usually not needed if the patio meets the R-Codes deemed-to-comply rules. The City offers a deemed-to-comply check for a fee.
  • Pergolas and shade sails: generally not needed, unless you're on the canals.

Setbacks come from the City's planning scheme, the R-Codes, local planning policies and any detailed area plan for your estate. You must also keep the minimum open space and outdoor living area. If anything doesn't meet these rules, you need development approval first, before the building permit can be decided.

What to send

  • A BA1 (with a Certificate of Design Compliance) or a BA2 form.
  • A site plan at 1:200 or better, showing setbacks to boundaries and all buildings, pools and retaining walls, plus existing and new ground levels.
  • A floor plan, section and elevations at 1:100 or better.
  • Construction details: materials, sizes, spans and spacing.
  • How it connects to the house and how it's anchored, such as footings.

The City prefers online lodgement through "Building Lodgements" on its website. Building Services is on 9550 3777. See the patio approval checker, shade sail approval and patio plans for council.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a permit for a patio in Mandurah?

Yes, unless it's freestanding, 10 m² or less and no more than 2.4 m high.

Do I need a permit for a pergola in Mandurah?

Not if it's freestanding, 20 m² or less and no more than 2.4 m high.

Does a patio need planning approval in Mandurah?

Usually not, if it meets the R-Codes deemed-to-comply rules. If it doesn't, you need development approval before the building permit.

Do canal homes in Mandurah have extra rules?

Yes. Pergolas and shade sails generally don't need planning approval, except on the canals, so check with a planning officer.
