City of Melville patio rules

Short answer

In Melville, every attached patio needs a building permit. A freestanding patio or shade sail needs one if it's over 10 m² or 2.4 m high; a pergola if it's over 20 m² or 2.4 m. Planning approval usually isn't needed if the patio is 3 m from the front street and open on two sides.

Last updated 13 November 2026

Building permit rules

StructureNeeds a building permit if
PatioAttached to the house (any size), or freestanding and over 10 m², over 2.4 m high, or in wind region C or D
PergolaOver 2.4 m high, over 20 m², or in wind region C or D
Shade sailAttached to the house (any size), or freestanding and over 10 m², over 2.4 m high, or in wind region C or D
Vergola (louvre roof)Treated as a patio

The City says a freestanding patio should be at least 900 mm from the house and 900 mm from the boundary, to meet the building code's fire rules.

Planning approval

In a residential zone, a patio usually doesn't need planning approval if:

  • It's set back 3 m from the front street and 1.5 m from a side street
  • The eaves are no higher than 3 m and the ridge no higher than 3.5 m
  • It's open on at least two sides, and open where it's next to a boundary
  • It covers no more than two-thirds of the main outdoor living area
  • Posts within 1 m of a side boundary are OK if all structures on that boundary total less than 9 m

Pergolas and shade sails don't need planning approval unless the property is heritage listed. In other zones, planning approval is always needed.

Fees and timeframes

  • Building permit under $20,000: $166.65. Over $20,000: 0.19% plus a 0.137% levy.
  • Verge bond for work over $20,000: $1,900 plus a $100 inspection fee.
  • Planning approval under $50,000: $147.
  • Decisions: BA1 in 10 working days, BA2 in 25. Planning in 60 days, or 90 with consultation.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a permit for a patio in Melville?

Yes if it's attached to the house. A freestanding patio needs one if it's over 10 m², over 2.4 m high, or in wind region C or D.

Does a pergola need approval in Melville?

Only a building permit if it's over 2.4 m high, over 20 m², or in wind region C or D. No planning approval unless the property is heritage listed.

How close to the front can a patio be in Melville?

3 m from the front street and 1.5 m from a side street without planning approval. Closer may be possible, so ask the City.

How much is a patio permit in Melville?

$166.65 for work under $20,000. Over that, 0.19% plus a 0.137% levy, and a $1,900 verge bond.
