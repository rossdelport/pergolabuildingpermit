City of Vincent patio rules

Short answer

In the City of Vincent, a patio needs a building permit unless it's freestanding, 10 m² or less and 2.4 m or lower. Planning approval usually isn't needed if it's 30 m² or less, 3.5 m or lower, out of the street setback and not heritage, or it fully meets the R-Codes.

Last updated 27 November 2026

Building permit

A patio needs a building permit unless it's freestanding, 10 m² or less and 2.4 m or lower. A patio attached to the house always needs one. Exempt patios still have to meet the building code, including fire separation. See the patio approval checker.

Planning approval

The City of Vincent says a patio doesn't need planning approval if it's on a lot with a house or grouped dwelling, isn't in a heritage-protected place, and either fully meets the R-Codes and the City's Built Form Policy, or meets the City's planning exemptions:

  • not in the front or secondary street setback,
  • no more than 30 m²,
  • no more than 3.5 m high,
  • floor no more than 0.5 m above ground, and
  • doesn't break the R-Codes rules for outdoor living area, open space and boundary walls.

Patios with apartments or shops on the lot, or that don't meet the R-Codes, need planning approval. The City has 60 days to decide (90 with consultation), and approval lasts 2 years.

Setbacks are measured to the posts, as long as eaves are no deeper than 0.75 m. Newer R-Codes Part C lots have similar rules. See patios on the boundary.

Raised patios and privacy

If the floor is more than 0.5 m above the ground, privacy rules apply. Usually the raised area must be 7.5 m from the boundary (R50 or lower) or have fixed screening at least 1.6 m high. See deck height rules.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a permit for a patio in the City of Vincent?

A building permit is needed unless it's freestanding, 10 m² or less and no more than 2.4 m high. An attached patio always needs one.

Does a patio need planning approval in Vincent?

Not if it meets the R-Codes and Built Form Policy, or the City's exemptions (up to 30 m², 3.5 m high, not in a street setback, not heritage).

Can a patio go on the boundary in Vincent?

Often yes, if it's no more than 10 m long and 2.7 m high, behind the front setback, with eaves and gutters at least 450 mm from the boundary.

Does a raised patio need screening in Vincent?

If the floor is more than 0.5 m above ground, it usually needs a 7.5 m setback (R50 or lower) or fixed screening at least 1.6 m high.
