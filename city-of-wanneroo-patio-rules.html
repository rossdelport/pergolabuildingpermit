City of Wanneroo patio rules

Short answer

In Wanneroo, every attached patio needs a building permit. A freestanding one doesn't if it's 10 m² or less and 2.4 m or lower. Keep posts 1 m from the side boundary (1.5 m if over 14 m long), or on the boundary if no longer than 10 m and no higher than 2.7 m. Expect a $140 verge bond fee too.

Last updated 2 November 2026

Do you need a building permit?

The City of Wanneroo calls a patio "an unenclosed structure covered in a water-impermeable material". Gazebos and thatched structures count as patios too.

  • No building permit if the patio is freestanding, 10 m² or less and 2.4 m or lower.
  • Every attached patio needs a building permit, whatever its size.

You only need planning approval (a development application) if the patio doesn't meet the standard R-Codes rules below.

Setbacks

WhereRule
Side and rear boundary1 m to the post for a patio 14 m long or less; 1.5 m if longer (the length includes structures next to it)
Posts on the boundaryAllowed if the patio is no longer than 10 m and no higher than 2.7 m, the roof is 500 mm back from the boundary, and it's behind the front setback
Front of the houseCan come forward to half the street setback: 3 m from the boundary on R20, 2 m on R30
Secondary street (corner lots)1.5 m
EavesCan project up to 0.75 m into a setback
Building code fire separation900 mm from the patio roof to the boundary, or 900 mm between a freestanding patio and the house

These apply to most R20 and R30 lots. Estates with a Local Development Plan can have different rules.

What to lodge

  • BA2 building permit application
  • Site plan at 1:200 with levels, setbacks, any septic system and a north point
  • Elevations and sections at 1:100
  • Construction details, including how it connects to the roof and the footings
  • Structural engineer's details
  • Street and Verge Bond application
  • Planning approval, if you needed one

Fees

The building permit is 0.32% of the value (at least $121), plus the $61.65 Building Services Levy for work up to $45,000. Wanneroo also charges a $140 verge bond admin fee. If the work is worth $20,000 or more, add a $2,200 refundable bond.

Contact

City of Wanneroo, phone (08) 9405 5000.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need council approval for a patio in Wanneroo?

Every attached patio needs a building permit. A freestanding patio doesn't if it's 10 m² or less and 2.4 m or lower. Planning approval is only needed if the patio doesn't meet the standard R-Codes rules.

How close to the boundary can a patio be in Wanneroo?

1 m to the post if the patio is 14 m long or less, or 1.5 m if longer. Posts can go on the boundary if the patio is no longer than 10 m and no higher than 2.7 m, with the roof 500 mm back.

What is the Wanneroo verge bond for a patio?

A $140 admin fee with every building permit, plus a $2,200 refundable bond if the work is worth $20,000 or more.

Can a patio go in front of the house in Wanneroo?

Yes, up to half the street setback: 3 m from the front boundary on an R20 lot or 2 m on R30, if it meets the other R-Codes rules.
