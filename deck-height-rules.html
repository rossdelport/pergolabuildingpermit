Question answer

How high can a deck be before it needs approval?

Short answer

A deck 500 mm or less above natural ground usually doesn't need planning approval. Above 500 mm it must meet setbacks and have a 1.6 m privacy screen if it's within 7.5 m of a side or rear boundary. A building permit may be needed at any height, depending on your council.

Last updated 15 November 2026

The 500 mm line

For planning approval, the number that matters is 500 mm, measured from natural ground level to the top of the deck. Natural ground is the level of the land before any digging or filling, not the height of your lawn after landscaping.

500 mm or lessNot needed

What a raised deck has to meet

Once a deck is over 500 mm, it can look into your neighbours' yards, so privacy rules kick in. The City of Joondalup's rules are a good example:

  • Privacy screen: if the deck is within 7.5 m of a side or rear boundary, it needs a fixed screen at least 1.6 m high above the deck surface.
  • Setbacks at R20: 1 m from side and rear boundaries for a deck 10 to 14 m long with a privacy screen; 1.5 m if it's longer than 14 m or has no screen.
  • Higher codes (R20/25 to R20/60): 1 m.

The City of Vincent uses the same 7.5 m distance or 1.6 m screen for raised floors over 0.5 m at R50 and below. Councils vary, and structure plans can add rules.

Building permit: councils differ

The building permit side isn't the same everywhere:

  • City of Wanneroo: every deck needs a building permit, whatever its height or size.
  • City of Joondalup: a deck needs a permit if it's over 20 m², higher than 500 mm, less than 900 mm from a boundary, in a bushfire-prone area, or has privacy screens.

So a deck that's 500 mm or higher will need a building permit in both. Check with your own council before you build.

Lower is simpler

If you can keep the deck at or under 500 mm, you usually avoid planning approval and privacy screens. If your block slopes, measure from natural ground at the highest point of the deck, because that's where it sits furthest above the land.

Questions

Common questions

How high can a deck be without council approval in WA?

Planning approval usually isn't needed for a deck 500 mm or less above natural ground. A building permit may still be needed: Wanneroo needs one for every deck, Joondalup for decks over 20 m², over 500 mm or within 900 mm of a boundary.

Where do you measure deck height from?

From natural ground level, which is the level of the land before any excavation or fill, to the deck surface.

Does a raised deck need a privacy screen?

In Joondalup, a deck over 500 mm within 7.5 m of a side or rear boundary needs a fixed screen at least 1.6 m high. Other councils use similar rules.

Can a raised deck go near the boundary?

At R20 in Joondalup, a raised deck needs 1 m from side and rear boundaries if it's 10 to 14 m long with a screen, or 1.5 m if longer or unscreened. Councils vary.
