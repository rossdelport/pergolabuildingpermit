Cost guide

How much does a deck permit cost in WA?

Short answer

For most backyard decks, the state fees come to about $182.65: the $121 minimum BA2 fee plus the $61.65 Building Services Levy. Plans, engineering and any planning approval cost extra. The City of Wanneroo says every deck needs a permit.

Last updated 12 November 2026

The state fees

Building permit fees are set by the state, so they're the same at every WA council:

ChargeRate
BA2 (uncertified)0.32% of the value, at least $121
BA1 (certified)0.19% of the value, at least $121, plus a private surveyor
Building Services Levy$61.65 for work up to $45,000

Examples

Deck value (incl. GST)BA2 feeLevyTotal

For most backyard decks, the minimum fee applies, because 0.32% only goes over $121 once the deck is worth more than about $37,800.

Other costs to budget for

  • Plans and engineering. Councils usually want a site plan, elevations and structural details.
  • Planning approval, if your deck needs it. The City of Wanneroo says it may be needed for decks higher than 500 mm above natural ground, and must come before the building permit. Melville charges $147 for planning approval on work up to $50,000.
  • Verge bond. Some councils, such as Wanneroo, charge a fee and bond for larger jobs ($140 plus a $2,200 bond for work of $20,000 or more).
  • Surveyor fee for a BA1.

Does every deck need a permit?

The City of Wanneroo says all decking needs a building permit, whatever its height or size. Councils vary, so check yours before you build.

Questions

Common questions

How much is a deck building permit in WA?

For most decks, about $182.65 in state fees: the $121 minimum BA2 fee plus the $61.65 Building Services Levy. Plans, engineering and any planning approval are extra.

Do I need a permit for a low deck in Wanneroo?

Yes. The City of Wanneroo says all decking needs a building permit, regardless of height or floor area.

When does a deck need planning approval?

In Wanneroo, it may be needed if the deck is more than 500 mm above natural ground. It must come before the building permit.

Is a BA1 cheaper than a BA2 for a deck?

The council fee is usually the same $121 minimum, but a BA1 also needs a private surveyor, who charges their own fee.
