Question answer

Does a patio need engineering in WA?

Short answer

Usually yes. If your patio needs a building permit, councils ask for engineering that shows the posts, beams, footings and fixings are strong enough. Standard patio engineering is advertised at about $350 to $450 in Perth, and most kits include it.

Last updated 20 November 2026

Why councils want engineering

A patio roof has to stand up to wind and its own weight. The engineering shows the posts, beams, footings and fixings are strong enough for your site. Without it, the council or building surveyor can't confirm the patio meets the building rules.

  • The City of Belmont asks for an engineer-certified structure with every BA2 patio application, plus a 1:200 site plan and 1:100 elevations.
  • For a BA1, the building surveyor's certificate (BA3) has to list the plans and technical documents that show the patio complies.
  • Perth patio kit sellers say engineering detail is required for a building permit application.

When you don't need it

If the patio is exempt from a building permit, you don't need to lodge engineering. In many Perth councils a freestanding patio up to 10 m² and 2.4 m high is exempt. Attached patios usually need a permit. Councils vary, so check yours. It still has to be built safely.
