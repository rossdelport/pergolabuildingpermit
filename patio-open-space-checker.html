Patio open space checker

Short answer

Under the R-Codes, a single house must keep 45% to 60% of the lot as open space, depending on the R-Code (50% at R20). A patio still counts as open space if it's open on 2 sides, no more than 0.5 m high, and up to 10% of the lot or 50 m².

House, garage, carport, sheds and other roofed areas

How open space works

The R-Codes say a set share of your lot must stay as open space, usually the area not covered by buildings. For single houses:

R-CodeMinimum open space
R1060%
R12.555%
R15 to R2550%
R30 to R4045%

The patio rule that helps

A patio or verandah can still count as open space if it's:

  • open (unenclosed) on at least 2 sides,
  • no more than 0.5 m above natural ground level, and
  • covering no more than 10% of the lot or 50 m², whichever is less.

Any patio area above that limit counts as covered. Pergolas without a roof always count as open space. Carports, garages and sheds never do.

Don't forget the outdoor living area

The R-Codes also want an outdoor living area next to your main living room (for example 30 m² at R20), with at least two-thirds of it without a permanent roof. A big patio over your only backyard space can fail this even if open space is fine.

If your patio meets these rules it usually doesn't need planning approval, but it may still need a building permit. Councils vary.

Check boundary rules in can a patio go on the boundary, try the patio approval checker, or read about enclosing a patio.

Questions

Common questions

Does a patio count as open space in WA?

It can. A patio open on at least 2 sides and no more than 0.5 m above ground counts as open space, up to 10% of the lot or 50 m², whichever is less.

How much open space do I need at R20?

At least 50% of the lot for a single house, plus an outdoor living area of 30 m².

Does a carport count as open space?

No. Covered car parking spaces don't count as open space under the R-Codes.

What if my patio leaves too little open space?

You'd usually need planning approval, or a design change such as a smaller patio, or one open on more sides.
