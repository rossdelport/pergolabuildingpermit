Cost guide

Patio prices in Perth (2026), approvals included

Short answer

Perth patio businesses advertise flat roof patios from about $250 to $400 per m² and gable patios from about $350 to $600+ per m². Kits start around $2,000. Add $182.65 in council permit fees for a typical patio, plus a verge bond at some councils.

Approvals on top

Most patios need a building permit: every attached patio at councils like Wanneroo, and any freestanding patio over 10 m² or taller than 2.4 m. The state-set council fees are:

Patio valueBA2 feeLevyTotal
$10,000$121$61.65$182.65
$20,000$121$61.65$182.65
$40,000$128$61.65$189.65

Add planning approval ($147 for work up to $50,000 at councils like Melville) only if your patio breaks the standard rules, such as in the front yard or too close to a boundary. Some councils add a verge bond; Wanneroo, for example, charges $140, plus a $2,200 refundable bond for work of $20,000 or more.

Is the permit in your quote?

Some builders include drawings, engineering and the permit; others leave it to you. Ask, and get it in writing. See how to check your patio builder is handling the permit.

Protections on bigger jobs

  • Contracts from $7,500 to $500,000 must be in writing, and the deposit can't be more than 6.5%.
  • Permit work over $20,000 needs home indemnity insurance.
  • Patios worth $50,000 or more need a registered builder.

Questions

Common questions

How much does a patio cost in Perth in 2026?

Perth patio businesses advertise flat roof patios from about $250 to $400 per m² and gable patios from about $350 to $600+ per m². One builder lists $8,000 to $12,000 for smaller flat roof patios and $18,000 to $25,000+ for large custom ones.

How much is a patio permit in Perth?

For a patio up to about $37,800 in value, the BA2 fee is the $121 minimum plus the $61.65 levy: $182.65 in total. Some councils add a verge bond.

Do patio prices include council approval?

Not always. Ask your builder whether drawings, engineering and the permit are included, and get it in writing.

Can I build a patio kit myself?

Yes. Since 1 July 2026, a homeowner can build a Class 10a structure like a patio worth under $50,000 without owner-builder approval, but it still needs a building permit if it's over the exemption limits.
