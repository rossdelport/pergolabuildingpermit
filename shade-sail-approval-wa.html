Question answer

Do you need council approval for a shade sail?

Short answer

Often, yes. A shade sail for a house needs no building permit only if it's 20 m² or less and 2.4 m high or less. Councils vary on the details. Joondalup needs a permit for any sail fixed to a building, and Belmont treats a waterproof sail as a patio. Planning approval usually isn't needed for a house.

Last updated 7 November 2026

The basic WA rule

WA's building rules let you put up a shade sail without a building permit if it's for a house, covers no more than 20 m², and is no more than 2.4 m high. Most shade sails over an outdoor area are higher than 2.4 m, so in practice many do need a permit.

Even when no permit is needed, the sail must still be built to the building code. Strong winds put big loads on posts and fixings.

Common traps

  • Fixing to the house. In Joondalup this alone means you need a permit.
  • Fixing to the fence. Joondalup says no part of a pergola or attached shade sail can be fixed to a dividing fence or hang over the neighbour's land.
  • Waterproof fabric. A sail that keeps rain out can count as a patio, which has tighter limits.
  • Height. The 2.4 m limit is easy to go over once you allow for head room and a sloped sail.

What you lodge if you need a permit

For an uncertified application (form BA2), councils usually want:

  • A site plan (at least 1:200) showing setbacks and easements
  • Elevations (at least 1:100)
  • Structural design certified by a structural engineer, covering the posts, footings and fixings

Joondalup has a specific checklist for pergolas and shade sails. Many shade sail installers include engineering in their quote, so ask.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need council approval for a shade sail in WA?

You need a building permit unless the sail is for a house, 20 m² or less and no more than 2.4 m high. Some councils, like Joondalup, require a permit for any sail fixed to a building.

Can I attach a shade sail to my house without a permit?

It depends on the council. In Joondalup, any shade sail attached to another building needs a building permit regardless of size. Check with your council.

Can I attach a shade sail to my fence?

Usually not. Joondalup says no part of a pergola or attached shade sail can be fixed to a dividing fence or encroach on neighbouring land.

Is a waterproof shade sail a patio?

In Belmont, yes. A shade sail with a non-permeable cover is treated as a patio, which is only exempt if freestanding and under 10 m².

Do shade sails need planning approval?

Usually not for a house. Belmont needs it in the front setback if the R-Codes aren't met, in heritage areas, special precincts or near the Swan River.
